Machine Learning

Under review · ICLR 2027

Basis-Adaptive Learned Asymptotic Nonlocal Inversion Neural Operator (BALANI-NO) for PDE Surrogates and Gravitational Lensing

P. Balani, P. Reddy, A. Batra, L. Paris, S. R. Mereddy, M. W. Toomey, S. Gleyzer

A spectral neural operator whose symbol adds a learned rational pole branch in |k| to a small low-mode table, making it grid-independent and axis-unbiased. Against ten baselines it has the lowest zero-shot error on Darcy flow, Navier–Stokes and every lensing split. Currently in the top 15% of submissions.

Pooling Discards Resolution-Invariant Structure: An Architecture and Ablation Study on Darcy Flow

P. Balani, P. Reddy, M. W. Toomey, S. Gleyzer

Zero-shot 16 → 32 resolution transfer across five operator architectures: FNO degrades 1.27× vs. 7.77× for a CNN, and removing pooling cuts degradation from 1.88× to 1.32× (UNO) and 2.12× to 1.35× (HC-UNO).
